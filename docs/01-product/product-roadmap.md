# Product Roadmap

Roadmap menunjukkan urutan pengembangan PETORA berdasarkan prioritas dan risiko.

## Phase 1 - Foundation

Authentication, role & permission, multi-branch, basic customer-pet, product master, inventory, POS, sales, payment, accounting baseline.

## Phase 2 - Service Operations

Booking, grooming, clinic, pharmacy, medical records, and supplier management.

## Phase 3 - Workforce & Finance

HR, attendance, payroll, commission, finance reconciliation, and reporting.

## Phase 4 - Customer Experience

Customer portal, marketing, loyalty, notification, and CRM.

## Phase 5 - Scale & Intelligence

Advanced analytics, e-commerce, forecast, and external integrations.

## Recommendation

Lakukan phased rollout by branch to minimize operational risk.
