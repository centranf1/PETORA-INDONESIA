# Glossary

Istilah kunci PETORA disusun untuk menjaga konsistensi terminologi di seluruh dokumentasi.

## Core Terms

Branch: lokasi operasional; Organization: perusahaan induk; Customer: pemilik/relasi pelanggan; Pet: hewan peliharaan; SKU: stock keeping unit; RLS: row level security; POS: point of sale; FEFO/FIFO: stock rotation logic; Shift: period of cashier work.

## Operational Terms

No-show, refund, settlement, stock opname, purchase order, service-level agreement, debit/credit journal, membership tier, and loyalty point.

## Security Terms

Service role, JWT, session, audit log, two-factor authentication, RBAC.

## Notes

Beberapa istilah dapat dipetakan ke field dan tabel tertentu sesuai skema database final.
