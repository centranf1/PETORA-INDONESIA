# Feature Backlog

Backlog berisi daftar fitur yang perlu dikelola di sprint, release, dan maintenance.

## Backlog Items

Authentication/authorization, branch context, warehouse stock movement, sales checkout, refund, purchase order, receiving stock, appointment scheduling, veterinary records, pharmacy prescription, staff payroll, finance postings, notifications, portal.

## Entry Criteria

Semua item backlog harus memiliki owner, acceptance criteria, and business rules before development starts.

## Priority Notes

Deferred items: e-commerce, advanced analytics, and integrated external shipping need management sign-off.

## Release Strategy

Release by domain: foundation, operations, service care, workforce, monetization, customer engagement.
